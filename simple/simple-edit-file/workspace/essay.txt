Failure is often seen as a sign of weakness, but it is one of the best ways to learn. 
Every mistake teaches us something that success alone cannot.
Many successful people have failed before reaching their goals.
What made them different was not their ability to avoid mistakes, but their willingness to learn from them and try again.
In the end, failure does not define who we are.
Our response to it does.
Those who accept their mistakes and use them to grow can turn their greatest setbacks into their greatest strengths.
